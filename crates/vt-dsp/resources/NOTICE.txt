Sonora is a Rust port of WebRTC M145 (branch-heads/7632).

All seven actual Sonora crates declare BSD-3-Clause:
sonora, sonora-aec3, sonora-common-audio, sonora-fft, sonora-simd,
sonora-ns and sonora-agc2, all version 0.2.0.
Their corresponding *-BSD-3-Clause.txt files reproduce the shared upstream
copyright/terms. sonora-aec3's published package inherits license.workspace
and does not package a separate LICENSE; its notice is the verified shared
repository/root Sonora LICENSE, identical to the six packaged license files.
Noise suppression and automatic gain control are disabled in vt-dsp at runtime;
we retain their notices because the Sonora wrapper depends on those crates.
